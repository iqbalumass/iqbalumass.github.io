About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Massachusetts Dartmouth. My research focuses on secure, robust, and trustworthy multimodal intelligence, with interests in computer vision, vision-language models, and adversarial machine learning.

I bring experience from academic research, university teaching support, and industry R&D. Before my doctoral studies, I worked at Samsung R&D Institute Bangladesh, where my responsibilities included software development and technical leadership.

I am pursuing a faculty career that brings together student-centered teaching, applied research, and mentoring. I enjoy helping students connect fundamental computer science ideas with problems they can visualize, implement, and investigate.
